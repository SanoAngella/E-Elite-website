Who We Are

Elite Medical Ltd is a leading medical devices wholesale company based in Kigali, Rwanda, dedicated to transforming healthcare through innovative surgical solutions.

We believe healing shouldn't come with pain, long scars, or endless recovery. That's why we specialize in advanced Minimally Invasive Surgery (MIS) — the modern solution for faster, safer, and smarter healing.

"All products are sourced from trusted international manufacturers and meet global medical standards. We partner with top global manufacturers to bring world-class equipment and consumables to hospitals, clinics, and surgical centers across the region."

Elite Medical delivers cutting-edge energy-based devices, including ultrasonic and advanced bipolar surgical equipment for advanced minimally invasive surgery, ensuring precision and superior patient outcomes.

Our Specialization

Elite Medical focuses on three core areas of medical supply excellence

🏥 Medical Equipment

Comprehensive range of hospital and clinical equipment including diagnostic devices, patient monitoring systems, and surgical tables from certified international manufacturers.

🔬 Minimally Invasive Surgery (MIS)

State-of-the-art MIS systems including HD visualization towers, laparoscopic instruments, energy-based surgical devices (ultrasonic and advanced bipolar), and complete endoscopy solutions.

High-quality surgical consumables and disposables including sterile gloves, gowns, sutures, endoscopy accessories, and specialized surgical supplies for all medical specialties.
